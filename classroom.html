Classroom Mode

Everything here runs locally in this browser — there's no backend, no student accounts, and nothing to register. Use a shared classroom computer, a projector, or your own laptop to plan lessons, share mission links, and build printable class reports from files your students export themselves.

Class roster

Names only, stored on this device — there are no student accounts. Use the roster to label printed handouts or match up imported progress files.

    Shareable mission links

    Every mission has a direct link, handy for putting a specific mission on the projector. Signed in as a teacher, you can open any mission. Students can only open a mission once they've unlocked it.

    Class report

    Ask each student to export their progress from their own Dashboard, then load all of those files here to build one printable class report — no server, no accounts.
