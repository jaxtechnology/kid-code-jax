Why Kid Code Jax

Everything you need to actually learn to code, nothing you don't.

No sign-up, ever

Every mission, badge and lesson works the moment the page loads. Progress saves to your own browser — nobody's servers, nobody's data.

Pick your character

Play as a jaguar, a puppy, a pirate, and more — same 50 missions, your own look. Switch anytime from your dashboard.

Safe by design

Every loop has a built-in limit, so a mistake just means trying again — never a frozen screen or a lost afternoon.

Built for the classroom

Teachers get a roster, shareable mission links, and printable class reports built from exported progress files — no accounts, no procurement.

Gamified, not gimmicky

XP, levels and streaks track real skill milestones — completing a loop challenge, clearing a sector, working hint-free — not just clicks.

Offline-friendly

Once loaded, missions keep working without a connection. Perfect for spotty school wifi or a laptop cart with no internet at all.
